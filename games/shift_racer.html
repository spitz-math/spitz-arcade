SHIFT RACER

Translations of linear functions: up, down, left and right. You have 5 minutes: answer to fuel up, then drive to as many checkpoints as you can.

1. AnswerRight answers add fuel and cash. 2 in a row unlocks 4th gear, 4 in a row 5th. A wrong answer costs $5 and locks you back to 3rd.
2. DriveHold ↑ to drive, ← → to change lanes. Press Space to shift when the needle hits green. Dodge traffic close for bonus points.
3. UpgradeSpend cash in the Shop on a faster engine, a bigger fuel tank, a smoother gearbox and power-ups.
Keys: 1–4 answer · Enter next · ↑ drive · ← → lanes · Space shift up · S shop · N music · M mute · Shift+R new game · F full screen
