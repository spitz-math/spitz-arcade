SHIFT RACER

Translations of linear functions: up, down, left and right. You have 5 minutes: answer to fuel up, then drive as far as you can.

1. AnswerEach right answer adds fuel, earns cash and shifts you up a gear. A wrong answer costs $5 and drops you to 1st gear.
2. DriveHold the road to drive and slide to change lanes. Dodge traffic and grab stars. Farther is more points.
3. UpgradeSpend cash in the Shop on a faster engine, a bigger fuel tank, star bonuses and power-ups.
Keys: 1–4 answer · Enter next · ↑ or Space drive · ← → steer · S shop · M mute · F full screen
