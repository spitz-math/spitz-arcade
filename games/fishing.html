GO FISH

Slope-intercept form, point-slope form, graphs, HOY and VUX. You have 5 minutes: answer to earn bait, then drop your line deep for the rarest fish.

1. AnswerEach right answer earns bait and cash. Every fish you hook takes 1 bait. A wrong answer costs $5.
2. DropPress Space to drop your line. Dodge fish on the way down to go deep. On the way up, steer into fish to hook them.
3. UpgradeSpend cash in the Tackle Shop on a longer line, more bait, better prices and power-ups.
