SPLAT RANGE

Slope-intercept form, point-slope form, graphs, HOY and VUX. You have 5 minutes: answer to load paint, then clear the stages.

1. AnswerEach right answer loads paint and earns cash. A wrong answer costs $5 and breaks your streak.
2. ShootEach shot uses 1 paint. Juggle cans, smash bottles, hit discs and Rivals (not Buds!). Beat each stage’s goal.
3. UpgradeSpend cash in the Shop on bigger splats, more paint, score multipliers and power-ups like Bomb Shots.
